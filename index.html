Term spread: 2s10s

Real rate: 10Y yield minus CPI

Between Jul-25 and Jul-26, the real rate (10Y minus CPI) fell from 4.8% to 2.4%, mostly because of rising inflation: CPI went up from 1.6% to 4.45%, while the 10-year yield rose only 40 bps. This favours borrowers but not bond investors, whose real return has shrunk. RBI hiked the repo rate by 25 bps in October 2026, shifting its stance to calibrated tightening.

Methodology and sources

  • CPI: MOSPI, all-India combined, YoY %. Final values; latest month provisional. Series rebased to 2024=100 from Jan-26 (previously 2012=100).
  • WPI: Office of the Economic Adviser (DPIIT), YoY %. Series rebased to 2022-23=100 in Jun-26.
  • G-Sec curve and yield history: RBI, month-end yield of SGL transactions in government dated securities.
  • Short-end yields: FBIL G-Sec par yield curve, semi-annual, 29-Sep-2026.
  • Policy rates: RBI MPC statements. SDF = repo − 25 bp; MSF = repo + 25 bp.
  • 2s10s spread = 10Y − 2Y yield, in basis points. Real rate = 10Y yield − CPI YoY (ex-post).
